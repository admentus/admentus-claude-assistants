# The interview

Fifteen questions in five parts. Ask them one at a time, in this order, in
plain language. Skip any question the head start (their website or pasted
text) already answered well, and say so: *"Your site says you work mostly
with manufacturers, so I'll skip that one."*

## How to ask

- **One question per message.** Wait for the answer.
- **One follow-up at most,** and only when the answer is too vague to write
  down usefully. "It depends" gets *"What's the most common case?"*
- **Their words, not yours.** Save what they said. Don't polish it into
  marketing language.
- **"Skip" is always fine.** Write "not answered" and move on.
- **Save after every answer** to `knowledge/interview-notes.md` before asking
  the next question (format below). If the session ends, nothing is lost.
- **Keep time.** After part 3, say roughly how much is left: *"Two short
  parts to go, about five minutes."*

## Part 1: You and your work

1. **"What's your name, your role, and the name of your firm?"**
2. **"In a sentence or two, what do you do and who do you do it for?"**
3. **"What do you sell? List your main services or offers, and roughly how a
   typical engagement works."** (Pricing is optional. Say so: *"Leave out
   prices unless you want Claude to know them."*)

## Part 2: Your clients

4. **"Describe your best client. What kind of business or person, what size,
   what situation are they in when they call you?"**
5. **"Who isn't a good fit for you?"**
6. **"How do clients usually find you?"** (for example: referrals, LinkedIn,
   events, a partner)
7. **"When someone picks you over others who do similar work, why?"**

## Part 3: Your week

8. **"What are you focused on this year? One to three things."**
9. **"Walk me through a normal week. Where does your time actually go?"**
10. **"What's the part of your week that only you can do, and wish you
    didn't?"**

## Part 4: Your people

11. **"Who should Claude know about? Your key clients, partners and referral
    sources: a first name and one line each is plenty."**

    Before they answer, repeat the privacy rule in one line: *"First names
    and a line of context only. No account numbers, health details or
    anything confidential."*

## Part 5: How you write and how you want Claude to work

12. **"Paste three to five things you've written that sound like you:
    emails, LinkedIn posts or messages. Short is fine."**
13. **"Are there words or phrases you use a lot? Any you never want to sound
    like?"**
14. **"What do you use for email, calendar, contacts or CRM, files and
    accounting?"**
15. **"How do you want Claude to work with you? Short or detailed answers?
    Ask questions first, or just take a first pass? Anything it should never
    do, or always check with you before doing?"**

## Saving answers: knowledge/interview-notes.md

Create it on the first answer with this heading, then append each answer:

```markdown
# Interview notes

Raw answers from the Claude Brain interview, saved as they were given.
The brain files are written from these. Delete this file if you'd rather not
keep it.

## <date>

### 1. Name, role, firm
<their answer, as given>

### 2. What you do and for whom
<their answer, as given>
```

Mark anything taken from their website or pasted text with "(from your
website)" or "(from what you pasted)", and only after they've confirmed it.

**Resuming:** if `knowledge/interview-notes.md` already exists, read it, tell
the user which part they reached, and continue from the first question with
no answer.
