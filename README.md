# Assistants for Claude, from Admentus

Small assistants for Claude's Cowork mode. Each one does one job and drafts
for you. None of them sends, posts or accepts anything on your behalf.

I'm Jeff Chastain. I build these for the people I work with, and I run bigger
versions of them in my own practice. Claude is the tool I use. If you use
something else, ask me and I'll point you the right way.

## Add them to Claude

1. In the Claude app, open **Customize**, then **Browse plugins**, then the
   **Personal** tab.
2. Click **+**, choose **Add marketplace from GitHub**, and enter
   **admentus/assistants**.
3. Install the assistant you want, then follow its setup.

You need a paid Claude plan that includes Cowork (Pro or above) and the Claude
desktop app (claude.ai/download). The LinkedIn assistants also need Google
Chrome with the Claude in Chrome extension, signed in to LinkedIn, because
they read LinkedIn in your own Chrome.

## The assistants

- **LinkedIn Inbox Assistant** (`linkedin-inbox-assistant`). Checks your LinkedIn messages three times a weekday, tells you who wrote and who is waiting on you, and drafts a suggested reply for each. Never sends anything. Needs Google Chrome with the Claude in Chrome extension. It reads LinkedIn by working your Chrome, which LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits, so setup shows a warning and asks you to confirm you understand the risk before it reads anything.

## ⚠️ The LinkedIn assistants

Any assistant here that reads LinkedIn does it by working your browser for
you. LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits bots and automated tools, and
LinkedIn does restrict accounts it catches using them. Each of these shows you
a full warning during setup and won't touch LinkedIn until you confirm you
understand the risk. They only read, they check on a light schedule, and they
stop the moment LinkedIn shows a sign-in or security page.

If LinkedIn is central to your income and you can't afford a restriction,
don't turn them on.
